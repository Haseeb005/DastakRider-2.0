# HEATMAP CALCULATION

Create a Heatmap Service.

The service should calculate demand scores every 2 minutes.

Demand should consider:

## Waiting Orders

Orders waiting for rider assignment.

Weight: 40%

---

## New Orders

Orders created during the last 15 minutes.

Weight: 25%

---

## Restaurant Density

Number of active restaurants in that zone.

Weight: 10%

---

## Historical Demand

Average order volume for the same weekday and hour.

Weight: 15%

---

## Rider Availability

Number of available riders.

Subtract score when many riders already exist.

Weight: -20%

---

0 and 100
